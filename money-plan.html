Everything here stays on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere — this file has no server behind it.

If something does not apply to you, put 0 or leave it blank. Both mean the same thing here — never type "NA" or a dash, because the app reads these as numbers and text turns into a silent zero you did not intend.

Zero is real information, not a gap. No term cover, no investments, no loans — the app treats each of those as a finding and tells you what it means, rather than skipping over it.
